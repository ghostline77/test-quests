# Heavy Metal — player route

1. After Act 1, receive Regina's Kabuki Cyberpsycho report. Reply **On my way.**
2. Follow the Cyberpsycho marker to the Kabuki site. Entering the outer area
   activates Voss's community; entering the reveal area, scanning, detection or
   combat reveals the encounter. He uses the fitted mantis blades as his combat
   weapon. There is no pre-fight conversation.
3. Neutralize Voss. Either a lethal or nonlethal result advances the quest and
   records its own outcome. Player defeat follows normal game death/reload.
4. Receive and read **VOSS // SERVICE DENIED**, describing rejected repairs and
   involuntary blade deployment. Evidence is granted after resolution so it is
   available for both outcomes.
5. Leave the area. At 70 metres the encounter community is cleaned up.
6. Regina asks for Voss's condition. V reports the recorded lethal/nonlethal
   outcome, then chooses **Sending you the log.** or **Who signed off on those
   implants?** Regina replies, payment is granted and the journal completes.
